## About This Knowledge Base

This is my second and final course project, and I chose to talk about the English Premier League. Its purpose is to give football fans and new readers a clear introduction to the competition through connected articles.

## Scope

The site contains 20 content articles in six categories: History, Competitions & Rules, Clubs, Players, Records, and Hall of Fame. To enable me to address the topic from multiple angles, which will help the viewer.

## Organisation

Each category has its own folder that gathers all the category's files. The homepage links to every category, and each category's index page links to its articles.

## Sources
My main sources are the Official Premier League website and the clubs' official websites.
