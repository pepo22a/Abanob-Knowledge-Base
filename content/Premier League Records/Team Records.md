## Incredible Teams' Achievements 

- A 100-point league season by Manchester City, 2017/2018
- A season without a league defeat by Arsenal, 2003/2004
- Thirteen Premier League championships by Manchester United, 1992/93–2012/13 
- A 98-point runner-up, Liverpool, 2018/2019

## Points and unbeaten records

Fans always question what achievement is the toughest: a single season without a defeat or a 100-point league. A draw keeps an unbeaten run alive, but earns only one point. A win earns three. An unbeaten team therefore does not automatically have the highest possible points total.
