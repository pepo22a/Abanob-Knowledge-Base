## The Invincibles season

Arsenal finished the 2003/04 Premier League campaign with **26 wins, 12 draws, and no defeats**. The team won the championship and became known as the Invincibles. Arsene Wenger was the manager, and Thierry Henry was their superstar.

## Chelsea Unbeaten Home Record

Jose Mourinho's Chelsea went unbeaten in his first 60 Premier League home matches at Stamford Bridge (46 wins, 14 draws) during his first stint, and extended his overall unbeaten home run in the Premier League to a record **77 matches** across both spells (including 15 wins and 2 draws in his second spell).
