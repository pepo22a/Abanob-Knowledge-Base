---
title: The Formation of the Premier League
date: 2026-10-03
---

The Premier League began in 1992, but English league football had existed long before that. The change just created a new organisation for the top division.

## Why a new league was created

The leading clubs wanted more control over commercial decisions. The Founder Members Agreement in July 1991 set out plans for independence in areas such as broadcasting and sponsorship. In February 1992, the 22 First Division clubs resigned from the Football League. The Premier League became a limited company in May, and its first season began on 15 August.

## How did the new system affect clubs' trophies?

When comparing trophies, always check the period being counted. A club’s total English league championships can include titles from before 1992. Its Premier League total only counts championships in the newer competition. That distinction helps readers compare clubs fairly.
