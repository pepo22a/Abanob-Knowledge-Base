## How did the Premier League Start?

The English Premier League officially began in August 1992. Top-flight clubs broke away from the Football League to gain commercial and broadcast independence. Brian Deane of Sheffield United scored the competition’s first-ever goal.

## The First Champions

Manchester United cemented its place in the league's history by winning the first two league titles in 1993 and 1994 under the leadership of star player Eric Cantona; they were followed by Blackburn, who won the league in 1995, led by their key star, Alan Shearer.

## From 22 clubs to 20

The first three seasons had 22 clubs. For 1995/96, the competition moved to 20. That meant each team’s league schedule changed from 42 matches to 38 because every club played each opponent twice.
