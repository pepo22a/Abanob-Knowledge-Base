title: The Premier League’s International Growth
date: 2026-10-03
---

With the start of the Premier League, the intense competition has attracted sports fans from across the globe, not just within the UK. Driven by the strength of the clubs and the presence of standout players whom fans are eager to follow, the league has become a staple of daily conversation among supporters worldwide.

## Broadcasting beyond England

The league’s published broadcast agreements for 2022–2025 covered territories across Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Americas. Examples included CANAL+ in France and Sky Deutschland in Germany.

## Players create connections

A useful way to understand international interest is to follow players as well as clubs. Mohamed Salah gives readers a connection between Egyptian football and Liverpool. Thierry Henry provides another route into learning about Arsenal’s history.
