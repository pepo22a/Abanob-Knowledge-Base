## Points System

Firstly, to understand how the standings work, we have to understand how the points system works. It's very simple: a win gives 3 points, a draw gives 1 point, and a loss doesn't give any points. For example, if a club has 3 wins, one draw, and 2 losses, that means the team has **10 points** from 5 games.

## How to read the standings columns

- **P:** Matches played.
- **W, D, L:** Wins, draws, and losses.
- **GF and GA:** Goals scored and goals conceded.
- **GD:** Goal difference, calculated as GF minus GA.
- **Pts:** Total points.

## What happens if two teams have the same number of points?

If two teams have the same number of points, the first thing to separate them is goal difference. If they have the same goal difference, then we look at goals scored, then head-to-head results, and finally a play-off game.
