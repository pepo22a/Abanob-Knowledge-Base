## What is VAR?

VAR stands for **Video Assistant Referee**. It is a system in which another qualified official uses video footage to help the referee deal with particular match-changing incidents. Some American sports have had similar systems for ages, but it wasn’t until 2019 that the Premier League introduced it.

## Which decisions can be reviewed?

- Goals and incidents directly connected to them.
- Penalty decisions.
- Direct red-card incidents.

We need to know that VAR does not review every single foul; intervention normally concerns an obvious error or a serious missed incident.

## How a review works

The video official checks footage and communicates with the referee. For some subjective decisions, the referee can review the incident on a pitch-side monitor. The **on-field referee retains the final decision**.

## What do fans think of VAR?

Fans' opinions are different about VAR; some fans think it guarantees fairness to the game; on the other hand, there are fans who think it slows down the rhythm of the game and kills the enjoyment.
