## How can teams participate in the Premier League?

The 20 Premier League spots are earned throughout the season through results. Every single season, the 3 teams with the lowest number of points get relegated to the Championship, which is the second tier of English soccer. On the other hand, the 3 teams with the highest number of points in the Championship earn promotion to the Premier League, which is the top division in English soccer.

## The Championship

The first two teams earn automatic promotion, while the third place is decided through the play-offs. Teams from 3rd place to 6th place have to face each other in semi-finals, then a final game to determine the 3rd team to be promoted. The final game of the play-offs has the highest money prize in the club soccer world.

## What is the importance of this system?

This system makes things very tense and entertaining on both ends of the standings table. Not only is the title race entertaining, but also the relegation battle at the bottom end of the table. This system also creates a link between different divisions of English soccer.
