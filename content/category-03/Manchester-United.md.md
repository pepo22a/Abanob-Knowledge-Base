
# Manchester United

Founded in 1878 as Newton Heath Football Club, before being renamed Manchester United in 1902, the club stands as one of England's most prestigious and successful teams. This is particularly true of the Premier League era, which began in 1992 under the leadership of Sir Alex Ferguson, the most renowned figure in the club's history, who secured 13 Premier League titles, a record for any manager. 

## Manchester United Rivals

United’s total league titles stand at 20, tying them with their arch-rivals, [[Liverpool.md]]. They also boast three UEFA Champions League trophies, compared to the single title held by their cross-town rivals, [[Manchester-City.md]]. 

### Munich Air Disaster

Known as the **"Red Devils,"** the team plays at the historic Old Trafford stadium, famously dubbed the _**"Theatre of Dreams."**_ Despite suffering a tragic plane crash in the early years of their domestic dominance, which claimed the lives of eight players, the club successfully rebuilt the squad and returned to competitive contention. United’s home kit consists of a red shirt and white shorts, and the club commands one of the largest fan bases in the world.
