# Manchester City

Manchester City stands as one of the modern powerhouses of English football. Although founded in 1880, the club did not emerge as a dominant force in the English league until 2008, when Sheikh Mansour, through his Abu Dhabi United Group, acquired the club and injected significant capital to drive its success. This investment paid off: while the club had won only two league titles before the takeover, it has secured eight since, along with a UEFA Champions League title. Manchester City’s sky-blue kit is one of the most iconic in English football, and the team plays its home matches at the Etihad Stadium. Its rivalry with [[Liverpool.md]] and [[Arsenal.md]] from 2017 to the present day ranks among the most thrilling and significant eras in the league's history. In recent times, the club has boasted a roster of exceptional players, most notably:

1. Kevin De Bruyne
2. Sergio Agüero
3. Erling Haaland

Most of Manchester City's recent successes came under the management of the legendary Pep Guardiola, who said on his unveiling day
>“What I want is after the game, after the season, for our supporters and those who love football to enjoy it. That is the most important thing. After that maybe we will win titles.”
