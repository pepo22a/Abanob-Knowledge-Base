# Liverpool 


is one of England's most successful clubs; founded in 1892 in the city of Liverpool, it has played its home matches at Anfield since its inception. The club is known as **"The Reds"**, a nickname derived from the all-red kit worn since the 1960s, and its motto, _**"You'll Never Walk Alone,"**_ is among the most iconic in the world. The club has enjoyed several periods of domestic dominance, beginning with its transformation into a major force in English football under the legendary manager Bill Shankly. This legacy of success continued with numerous domestic and European achievements under 
## distinguished managers:

- Bob Paisley 
- Kenny Dalglish
- Jürgen Klopp.

Although the Premier League era has not been Liverpool's most dominant period, with only two titles since the league's inception in 1992, the club boasts a total of 20 league titles, tying it with its traditional rival, [[Manchester-United.md]]. However, Liverpool holds the edge in European competition, having won six UEFA Champions League titles compared to Manchester United's three.
