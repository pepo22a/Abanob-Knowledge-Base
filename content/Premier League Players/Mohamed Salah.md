Mohamed Salah’s Premier League story is closely connected with Liverpool. The Egyptian "King" -as the fans love to call him- joined the club in 2017 and produced an exceptional first league season, giving supporters an immediate reason to follow his career.

# Mohamed Salah’s Premier League Impact

Salah's debut season at Liverpool arguably is the best in Premier League history. Salah scored **32 Premier League goals** in 2017/18 and won the Golden Boot. At that time, his total set a new scoring record for a 38-match Premier League season.

## Salah's Attacking Role

Salah is an example of why a forward’s starting position does not tell the whole story. An attacker can begin in a wide area and still become a major goalscorer by moving into central scoring positions.
