## Saka's Early Days

Bukayo Saka’s path into the Premier League began through Arsenal’s academy. Bukayo Saka’s development from his early days in the academy to becoming one of the Premier League’s standout stars serves as a textbook example of how to nurture young talent and help players reach their full potential.

## Bukayo's Arsenal Debut 

Saka made his Premier League debut against Fulham on **1 January 2019**, aged 17. He came on as a substitute in Arsenal’s 4–1 win and became the first player born in 2001 to appear in the competition.

## Becoming superstar

Following Arsene Wenger's departure from Arsenal and the appointment of Mikel Arteta as manager, Saka transformed from a player filling multiple positions with limited playing time into the team's winger, its premier star, and the focal point of Arteta's project. A shift from which Saka has greatly benefited.
