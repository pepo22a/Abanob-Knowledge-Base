## Erling Haaland’s Record-breaking Debut Season

Erling Haaland made an immediate impact after joining Manchester City in 2022. In his debut season, Haaland scored **36 league goals** in 2022/23, breaking the 34-goal Premier League record.

## Classic Number 9

Amidst the competition among clubs for forwards capable of playmaking and fulfilling multiple roles, Haaland has emerged to remind everyone of the importance of the classic striker—one who may not move extensively around the pitch yet is capable of scoring a massive number of goals from any position.

## Erling's Presence on Social Media

In addition to being a brilliant player, Erling Haaland is active on social media; his sense of humor makes him highly popular among young people and fans alike, fostering a close bond with his supporters.
