Alan Shearer is one of the biggest legends in English soccer. He had an exceptional career with fantastic goal-scoring ability; he played for more than 20 years and finished as the Premier League top scorer with **260 goals**, representing two Premier League clubs: Blackburn and Newcastle United.

## Alan Shearer's Blackburn Career


Shearer spent four seasons with Blackburn Rovers, making 171 appearances and scoring 130 goals, 112 of which were in the English league. He played a key role in Blackburn winning the league title in 1995, scoring 34 goals that season, which was the record at the time.

## Alan Shearer's career with Newcastle United

Alan Shearer played 405 matches for Newcastle United, scoring 206 goals, including 148 in the Premier League before retiring there and subsequently managing the team.

## Hall of Fame Recognition 

Shearer and Thierry Henry were the first two Hall of Fame inductees in 2021. Their careers also show different routes to recognition: Alan Shearer developed within the English league and never left it throughout his career, embodying consistent performance over time; in contrast, Thierry Henry emerged outside the English league before moving to Arsenal, where he reached a level that led people to describe it as the greatest "prime" a player has ever experienced in the league's history.
