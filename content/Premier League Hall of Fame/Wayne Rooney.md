Wayne Rooney began his Premier League career at Everton at the age of 17; his standout performances at such a young age earned him the "Golden Boy" title. He subsequently moved to Manchester United, embarking on a historic tenure with the club that concluded with him becoming its all-time leading goalscorer.

## Rooney's Premier League career 

Rooney finished with **208 Premier League goals** and **103 assists** across 491 appearances. He won five Premier League titles with Manchester United. he was a key player in all five Premier League titles he won. His ability to create chances as a striker was revolutionary at that time and opened the door to a different kind of striker.

## Premier League Recognition 

Rooney was inducted into the Premier League Hall of Fame in 2022. The honour makes his profile a useful starting point for readers discovering notable retired players.
