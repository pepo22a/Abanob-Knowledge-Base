Thierry Henry is arguably the most famous player in Arsenal's history, and arguably in the Premier League's history. His legendary tenure at the club earned the admiration and respect of all fans, including those of rival teams.

## Arsenal Career

Henry joined Arsenal from the Italian club Juventus in a deal worth approximately $12 million. He played 370 matches for the club, scoring 227 goals, including 174 in the Premier League, and won two league titles, one of which was achieved during the famous "Invincibles" season.

## Key point in Henry's Career

Wenger developed Henry from a winger into a striker. The league’s Hall of Fame profile highlights his ability to attack from the left as well as threaten closer to goal. That shows how important it is to play in the right position and in the right role for your abilities.

## Hall of Fame Recognition 

Shearer and Thierry Henry were the first two Hall of Fame inductees in 2021. Their careers also show different routes to recognition: Alan Shearer developed within the English league and never left it throughout his career, embodying consistent performance over time; in contrast, Thierry Henry emerged outside the English league before moving to Arsenal, where he reached a level that led people to describe it as the greatest "prime" a player has ever experienced in the league's history.
