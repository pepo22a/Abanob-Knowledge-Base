# Arsenal

Arsenal is one of the most historic clubs in southern England, specifically North London, founded in 1893. A fierce competitor in English football throughout the 20th century, the club is renowned for its red kit, its distinctive cannon-bearing crest, and its famous nickname, **"The Gunners."** Arsenal was the only major rival in the city until the turn of the millennium, when investment in Chelsea transformed that club into a formidable London adversary; [[Chelsea]] eventually surpassed Arsenal in European competition, winning two Champions League titles compared to Arsenal's zero. 

### The Invincibles

The tenure of manager **Arsène Wenger**, who led the team for 22 years, marks one of the club's most notable eras; under his leadership, and spearheaded by the club's ultimate legend, French player **Thierry Henry**, Arsenal achieved the historic **"Invincibles"** season in 2003–2004, winning the league title without a single defeat.
