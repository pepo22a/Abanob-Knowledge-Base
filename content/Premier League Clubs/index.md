# Premier League Clubs

English clubs rank among the strongest in the world, not only in terms of performance but also regarding purchasing power and the ability to attract top players. Even smaller English clubs can compete with major teams from other leagues to sign elite talent; this is largely due to the equal distribution of massive broadcasting revenues among the league's twenty participating clubs, a system that has significantly elevated the quality of competition. In this category, I will showcase the five biggest English clubs.
