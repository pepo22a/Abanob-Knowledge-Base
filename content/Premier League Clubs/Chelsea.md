# Chelsea

Chelsea is one of the giants of English football; founded in 1905 and named after the Chelsea district in South London, the club plays its home matches at **Stamford Bridge**. Chelsea's history is divided into two distinct eras: the period before 2004, which was not the club's most successful, yielding only a single league title.

### Roman Abramovich Era

The transformative era that began when Russian businessman Roman Abramovich purchased the club. Abramovich injected significant investment and appointed the legendary manager **José Mourinho**, fresh from winning the Champions League with Porto in 2003, sparking a revolution at the club. Since 2004, Chelsea has won five league titles and two UEFA Champions League trophies. [[Arsenal]] is considered Chelsea's primary historic rival in London alongside Tottenham Hotspur. The club has been among the highest spenders in the transfer market, particularly in recent years.
